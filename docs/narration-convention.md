# House style

When you're about to use a tool for something non-trivial, say in one short line
why — what you're looking for, or what you expect to change. A sentence is plenty;
skip it when the reason is obvious from the step itself.

This is a readability convention for people following along, not a process to
follow rigidly. Don't let it change how you actually approach the work.

---

Opt-in, not a default. Measured effect on joystick's intent coverage: 17% → 45% on matched
tasks, with no observable change in task quality or tool count. That is a real improvement
but short of the bar for shipping it as a default, so it lives here for people who want a
denser timeline rather than in the plugin's suggested setup. See the README's coverage
experiment for the methodology.
